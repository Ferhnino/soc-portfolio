Mustang Panda

Bottom line

Mustang Panda (also tracked as Stately Taurus and Earth Preta) is a China-nexus espionage group that relies on phishing lures, DLL side-loading through legitimate software, and a small set of well-known implants (PlugX, TONESHELL, PUBLOAD). The behaviors below apply to any organization, and several are cheap to hunt for. The one most worth checking this week is abuse of the legitimate code.exe tunnel feature to create a remote access channel.

Scope

Per this workflow's scoping rule, it includes only behavior relevant to US federal agencies or applicable to any entity, and it excludes activity specific to other countries. Two recent items in the feed (Cisco Talos, September 30, 2026, and SentinelOne, July 9, 2026) focus on Asia-specific targeting and were left out for that reason. This sample does not include IOCs.

Hunt ideas

  • VS Code tunnels. Look for code.exe launched with tunnel in the command line, especially on servers and on hosts where developers don't normally work.
  • Side-loading. Look for signed, legitimate executables running from unexpected paths such as user profile folders or temp directories, especially when they load an unsigned DLL from the same folder.
  • Discovery tooling. Look for AdFind, nbtscan, and Impacket artifacts on workstations, and for wevtutil run by non-administrative accounts.
  • Credential theft. Look for LSASS memory access from unusual processes and for DCSync-style replication requests from non-domain-controller hosts.
  • Exfiltration. Look for curl or FTP traffic to Dropbox and similar services from servers that don't normally reach them.

Starting point for the first idea (Microsoft Defender XDR table in Sentinel)

DeviceProcessEvents
| where FileName =~ "code.exe"
| where ProcessCommandLine has "tunnel"
| project TimeGenerated, DeviceName, AccountName, ProcessCommandLine, InitiatingProcessFileName
| order by TimeGenerated asc
